## The 12-point planning checklist

1. Define team size and expected attendance pattern.
2. List focus, collaboration, call, learning and visitor tasks.
3. Record accessibility and individual-adjustment needs.
4. Confirm usable floor area and fixed building constraints.
5. Map power, data, lighting and circulation constraints.
6. Rank workpoints by frequency and business importance.
7. Set a whole-project budget, not only a product budget.
8. Request dated product specifications and quotations.
9. Compare options using the same criteria and evidence.
10. Pilot the highest-risk assumptions with a small group.
11. Record human approvals, dependencies and rollback options.
12. Review feedback and actual usage before expanding.

## Questions for a supplier

- Which specifications support each material claim?
- What is included in delivery, installation and after-sales support?
- Which assumptions drive the proposed quantity and layout?
- What accessibility, safety or facility checks remain?
- What can be piloted before a wider commitment?

## AI prompt for a first-pass brief

> Using only the supplied team, task, space and budget facts, create three workplace-plan options. Separate facts, assumptions and evidence gaps. Do not invent wellbeing, productivity, environmental or compliance claims. Return the options, trade-offs, questions, human checks and recommended next action.
