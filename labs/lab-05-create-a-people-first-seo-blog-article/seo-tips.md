# SEO Tips — Lab 5: Create a People-First SEO Blog Article

## Five habits

1. Start with the user task and business outcome, not keyword count.
2. Give AI verified inputs and force unknown values to remain unknown.
3. Ask for evidence, uncertainty and alternatives—not only polished prose.
4. Review visible content, source code, mobile behaviour and accessibility together.
5. Keep a before snapshot, decision log, acceptance test and rollback path.

## Pitfall shields

- AI output is a draft or hypothesis until a named human verifies it.
- Search volume, difficulty, rankings and analytics must come from dated data.
- Structured data must match visible content and never guarantees a rich result.
- AI Overview inclusion cannot be guaranteed; foundational SEO remains the core.
- Earn relevant attention and citations; do not buy or automate links.
- Environmental, health, legal and financial claims need suitable evidence.
- Never expose API keys, credentials, customer data or confidential material.

## Acceptance reminder

> Every material claim is supported or qualified, the page resolves intent, adds original operational value and passes the six-part AI content QA scorecard.
