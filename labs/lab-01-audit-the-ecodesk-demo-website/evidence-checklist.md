# Evidence Checklist — Lab 1

- [ ] Inputs are public, approved or synthetic.
- [ ] Observations and AI inferences are separated.
- [ ] Metrics and claims have a dated source.
- [ ] Human reviewer is named.
- [ ] Accessibility, mobile and links were tested.
- [ ] Acceptance test: Every finding cites observable evidence, separates fact from inference, includes a verification method and maps to a business or user outcome.
- [ ] Before snapshot and rollback copy are retained.
